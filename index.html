Our Heritage

A courtly legacy, draped by hand

For centuries the royal ateliers of Marwar, Mewar and Amber dressed queens in cloth that took months to complete. Their weavers, dyers and printers passed every technique from parent to child.

Kridia works directly with those families, paying fairly and documenting every maker, so the saree you wear arrives with the name of the hands that made it.

  • Hereditary craft

    Woven, knotted and printed by families practising for generations.

  • Pure materials

    Silk, cotton, real zari and natural dyes, never blends.

  • Signed by the maker

    Each saree carries its artisan’s name and story.
